Mistral AI Services and Technologies

Mistral AI provides language models tailored to the needs of individuals, researchers, and businesses. These services rely on processing textual and multimodal data over high-performance network infrastructures.

MISTRAL AI

Operating Principles and Solutions

Generative artificial intelligence models do not read words directly: they break text down into tokens (chunks of words or characters). The cost and energy required depend on the amount of tokens processed by the model via interfaces or APIs.

AI ASSISTANT

Le Chat

Le Chat is Mistral AI's conversational assistant. It allows users to ask questions, summarize documents, rephrase text, or explain complex concepts. It can be used in various contexts: a student might use it to better understand a lesson, while a professional could ask it to synthesize a report. Its operation relies on models capable of producing responses based on the provided information and instructions. However, the answers are not always accurate and should be verified.

DEVELOPMENT

Studio & API

Mistral AI also offers solutions designed for developers who wish to integrate its models into their own applications. An API allows two software programs to communicate: an application can send a request to an artificial intelligence model and retrieve its response. For instance, a company can create an assistant capable of answering customer queries, automatically classifying messages, or helping employees search for information. Developers can test models and prepare their integrations using the tools provided by Mistral AI. API usage may incur costs depending on the chosen service and the volume of data processed.

DOCUMENTS

Document AI

Business documents often contain key information that needs to be extracted and organized: dates, amounts, names, or references. Automated document processing makes it possible to extract this data from digital files, especially when their formatting is complex. For example, a company can use this technology to streamline invoice or form processing. This helps limit manual tasks and saves time. Nevertheless, human verification remains necessary when dealing with critical data or hard-to-read documents.

AUDIO

Voxtral

Voxtral is a family of artificial intelligence models from Mistral AI dedicated to audio processing. These technologies enable speech-to-text conversion and streamline the processing of recordings. They are particularly useful for transcribing meetings, preparing reports, or finding information within recorded conversations. The goal is to reduce the time spent on manual transcription. However, the quality of the result depends on several factors, such as sound quality, background noise, and the number of speakers.
