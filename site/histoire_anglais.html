Mistral AI, an artificial story.

Mistral AI is a French company specializing in artificial intelligence. Founded in 2023, it quickly gained recognition through its language models.

Key Milestones


2023

The Founding

Mistral AI was founded in Paris by Arthur Mensch, Guillaume Lample, and Timothée Lacroix. The three founders notably come from artificial intelligence research backgrounds.

2023

The First Models

The company presented its first language models and began to draw attention within the artificial intelligence sector.

Following its creation, Mistral AI introduced its initial language models, which enabled text understanding and generation.

2024

Development

Mistral AI continued to expand its lineup, notably in 2024 with the Mistral and Mixtral families, alongside various services tailored for developers and enterprises.

This period marked a new milestone in the growth of Mistral AI.

A EUROPEAN AMBITION

A New Player in AI

From its inception, Mistral AI aimed to participate in the advancement of artificial intelligence across Europe.

The company strives to deliver high-performing models and develop its own proprietary technologies. Its expansion also helps strengthen the European presence in the artificial intelligence sector.

Mistral AI Today

TODAY

A Rapidly Evolving Company

Today, Mistral AI continues to advance its artificial intelligence models and services. The company notably offers Le Chat, a conversational assistant powered by its technologies.

Le Chat allows users to interact with artificial intelligence to get answers, write or edit text, search for information, or execute various tasks.

Mistral AI also continues to develop models designed for enterprise clients and developers.

These technologies can be integrated into diverse applications to automate tasks and improve information processing.
